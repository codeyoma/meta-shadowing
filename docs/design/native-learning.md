# Native learning surfaces

Mode: Operate. Scope: library, lesson/stages, player and settings.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A friendly spoken-practice app with one clear next action; the owner
approved Duolingo-inspired solidity, not the previous muted glass treatment.

OWN-WORLD: Use the owner's DESIGN.md role swaps: primary yellow #ffc800, pressed
orange #ff9600, Bee accent #58cc02, Fox accent #58a700, Macaw #1cb0f6, navy #042c60,
white, and structural grays. The app is 쇄도잉 and uses the supplied logo and icon. Raised 16-point
buttons, crisp borders, rounded display lettering and original lesson artwork.
Adapt ink/surfaces for contrast and dark mode rather than placing white on lime.

STORY: Choose a language and real book, install it, open its stage tab, and
practice. Confirmed completions drive actual local XP and streaks; never seed
fake rewards. Settings are a third persistent browsing tab.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Language flag, level/XP and flame/streak above the owner's full, uncropped 2:1 logo on a
white plate, a compact introduction and one illustrated Morning Notes package.
Content remains scrollable at larger type sizes. Stages are numbered selectable
rows with real checkpoint/completion data. The logo is capped at 260 points wide
to keep the first package action within the ordinary-size first viewport.

SIGNATURE: Raised buttons physically depress on touch without changing their
layout footprint. Learning progress fills only from actual confirmed state.

FORM: Owner-pinned Duolingo-inspired learning UI takes precedence over direction
seed decaaff6. Code-first implementation was explicitly approved; no comp applies.

REACH AND LIMITS: Reuse tokens and controls across all four screens; use native
tabs, with the player outside them and a language-selection sheet. Settings choices remain local and silent. Check light,
dark and larger type on iPhone. Playback, package validation, storage and stage
cycle rules stay unchanged. Daily XP rules are defined in `docs/learning-contract.md`;
full M1 device acceptance remains separate.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
